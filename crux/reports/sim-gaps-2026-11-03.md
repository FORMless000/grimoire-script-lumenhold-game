# Crux: win rate by level, at the same level and 10 or 20 below

Every encounter (61) at its own level, with the player at that level, 10 below and 20 below (at least level 1), with each of the 10 kits: 1830 cells.
- **Engine** (the smart AI): its search's forecast at the first move (depth 4), averaged over 4 shuffles a cell; **played**: 2 meetings a cell played out by it.
- **Bot** (the usual player): the smart bot over 16 meetings a cell. A win scores 1, a partial deal ½.
- Kits exist at levels 20, 50 and 80 only: a player at any other level gets the nearest one's skills and spells (35 and 65 are the steps).

## Findings

- **The curve is shaped by the kit presets more than by the gap.** Kits exist at 20, 50 and 80, so a player at 36–45 meets those encounters with a level-50 kit (engine 95%, bot 75% at the same level), while one at 26–35 has a level-20 kit (77% · 49%). Ten levels below a 46–55 encounter still has the 50 kit (80% · 58%); twenty below drops to the 20 kit (34% · 11%). The 56–65 encounters (mir_workshop_closed, grade_appeal, thesis_defence) are the hardest at the same level: 55% · 38%.
- **Gap cost** (engine played · bot): same level 83% · 60%, ten below 65% · 39%, twenty below 58% · 31%. The first ten levels cost about 20 points; the next ten about 7, because many of those players already sit on the level-20 kit floor.
- **Rapport is overpowered.** Leaving it out costs the bot 19 points on the Illusionist at tier 50 (39 cells × 16 meetings, the same seeds with and without), and both players cast it almost every meeting it's in the kit. It is the Illusionist's lead: the only tier-50 kit with Rapport, and the best kit there by 11 points (bot 68% against 43–57%).
- **The crux spells lead the kits.** The four kits with Starlight (Duellist, Scholar, Illusionist, Showman) are the top four by the bot's score. Starlight is worth 4–7 points to the bot and is the engine's most valued spell (forecast +14, cast in nearly every meeting).
- **Foresight carries tier 20.** It is worth about 10 points to the bot there (6–16 by kit); the three kits without it at level 20 (Brawler, Sailor, Underground tough) are the weakest at that tier, 32–33% against 42–45%.
- **Dead weight.** Lodestar, Seal, Brace, Reflect, Index, Ward, Shadowstep and Flame are worth about nothing to either player (Shadowstep and Flame are exits, never taken while winning is possible).
- **The engine's eval misreads Ward.** Leaving Ward out *raises* the engine's forecast by 21 points, though it casts Ward in 2% of meetings: an eval artefact (its features about which spells your Mana affords), not a game effect. The engine's spell worths are forecasts; trust the bot's, which are played.
- **The forecast is a rough guide only.** The engine's own meetings win more than its first-move forecast (83% against 75% at the same level). In the first run it read high at level 20. Use *engine played* for levels.

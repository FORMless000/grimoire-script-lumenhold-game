# Crux: simulated runs per encounter, level and kit

1830 cells: 61 encounters, each at its own level, against player levels 20 / 50 / 80 and 10 kits.
- **Engine** (the smart AI): the search's value at the first move to depth 4 (or until solved), averaged over 4 shuffles a cell. One meeting a cell was also played out by the engine, to check the forecast.
- **Bot** (the usual player): the smart bot's score over 8 meetings a cell (a win 1, a partial ½).

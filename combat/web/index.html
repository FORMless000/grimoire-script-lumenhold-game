Main game around the fight
Entering from the main game

What the main game hands a fight: blank means fit, full and the budget's purse. Health 30 or more starts at full HP; below that the start falls to 70% at Health 0. Mana under the fight's capacity starts as flux. Health is lost only if you end below 30 HP.

Adjust the experiment

Named peers keep their worldbook skill shape at a mean skill of the level. Faculty and villagers use their fixed worldbook skills and level. Raising the level never removes a spell. Random picks one of the general loadouts.

Easy and Medium play their character (persona opponents with readable habits); Hard and Engine search 5 exchanges ahead, Hard settling for weaker moves it likes. Encounter seeds reproduce random loadouts; AI seeds reproduce AI choices.

Magic changes hands.

Three basic actions and every learned spell. Commit in the open; both actions resolve together. A knockout, a concession or a stalemate ends the fight.

Two thirds of the Mana you cast becomes flux. Refocus takes it back.

# Event classes

> Every event declares `class:` and `tone:`; lint checks the class budgets with the preview engine.

| Class | What it is | Rule | Measured |
|---|---|---|---|
| `routine` | everyday life; light, quick, a small trade-off | no option obviously best, each within ±1.5 medium-equivalents (`costs.md`). A free option is fine beside one that gives something else. A routine card tagged `placeholder` (a stand-in for content still to write) is listed by lint instead of judged | per event |
| `milestone` | friendships, confessions, promotions; earned, and given room to breathe | may be pure reward; capped at 2 large | per run: count and value |
| `incident` | something goes wrong | may be all-negative; must offer agency (mitigate vs accept) | per run: count |
| `windfall` | good luck; small and funny | may be all-positive; capped at 1 large | per run: count |
| `story` | main-plot and arc beats; tense or eerie, never jokey at the climax | exempt from the budget; reviewed by the critic. Only hard-canon story events `find` a key clue or `advance` an arc clock | per arc |
| `crisis` | | exempt | rate per play style |

**Tones** (`@vocab tones`): light, warm, tense, grim, eerie. The director avoids two cards of the same tone or primary tag in a row.

**Weights** (how often an eligible event is drawn, against the others it competes with; the default is 10): a milestone weighs more than the cards around it, so it appears soon after it unlocks; a niche event weighs less.

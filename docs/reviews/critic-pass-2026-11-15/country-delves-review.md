The remaining structural concern is narrower: `gy_clues` is a hand-maintained plot-engagement counter, contrary to the explicit no-counter rule in `docs/authoring/costs.md` and AGENTS.md. The maps plan approves a side story and leads, but contains no exception to that rule. A dedicated side arc would keep it independent of the Hollow Star; alternatively an intended exception needs an explicit recorded design deviation. This is a rule finding, not a claim that the existing counter fails to increment.

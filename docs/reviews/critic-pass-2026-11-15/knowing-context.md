## Shared wiring and knowledge semantics

- [`knowing.grim`](../../../content/events/knowing.grim:8) defines `@pool knowing`: `class: routine`, `repeat: 1`, `tags: people facts`; it does not define a trigger. Direct fact-payoff events supply `trigger`, `place`, or actor themselves. [`@pool second_hand`](../../../content/events/knowing.grim:14) is `trigger: social`, `class: routine`, `repeat: 1`, `tags: people facts`.
- Most direct fact cards gate a choice with `when: knows(fact)`; `know_nessa_planted` and `know_tamsin_failed` use `if knows(fact)` and retain a separate else outcome. The source cards do not `learn` their own gated fact. Second-hand cards are the reverse: their `when` usually checks `not knows(target_fact)`, and both options execute `learn target_fact`.

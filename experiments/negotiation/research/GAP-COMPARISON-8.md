# Half-steep skill gaps · paired comparison

6912 matched games. Every encounter/player/resource/spell setup matches after normalizing only the version label and levelGapScale. Identical seeds and fixed search settings were used. Full wins only; partial agreement counts as failure. Each cell has 96 games sharing 48 seed clusters across two contexts.

The chance formula changes only the named-signal minus encounter-DC term from 1.3 to 0.65 percentage points per point. Card difficulty variation and Agreement targets retain their previous scaling. Winning probabilities need not become exactly half as steep because of clamps, resource choices, repeated checks and target thresholds.

Lumenhold / research prototype 03 · pressure

The Conversation

Confront the requests you can afford. Deflect the ones you cannot.

Two contextual responses. All twelve spells stay available. Gold is never rebated; bribery is offered only in the broker encounter. No Mana flux.

How to play

Earn four Agreement before eight Patience runs out. Suspicion at six ends the meeting. Confront gains one Agreement, spends one Patience and raises pressure. Deflect gains no Agreement, spends two Patience and lowers pressure by two. Each request supplies contextual response names and exact costs based on your skills.

Cast at most one spell before your response. Casting uses no Patience, pays Mana and immediately raises Suspicion. Foresight reveals the next three requests. All twelve spells remain available. The player AI searches with a 50 ms cooperative budget.

# T2 more region cards: author's notes

Batch: `content/events/maps/region_streets_2.grim` (37 cards: 20 capital streets, 17 countryside lanes). No worldbook changes, no new proper nouns, no tests (the pools' wiring is T1's, tested by `test/away.test.js`).

## Context

- The plan (`docs/maps-plan.md`, round 2, F) wanted about 30 `region: capital` and 25 `region: countryside` cards; T1 left 10 and 7. With these, 30 and 24.
- The cards join T1's pools `capital_streets` and `country_lanes` (`region_streets.grim`), so the wiring is said once there: no place, `class: routine`, weight 8, drawn by the away plans' social and world slots and as a visit's fallback once a place's own cards are spent. Once each.
- **Seasons:** breaks are deep winter and high summer, but an internship away can fall in any month, so seasonal cards say so (`season == "winter"`: chestnuts, muffin men, the hedger, wassailing, mummers; `season == "summer"`: the water cart, the heatwave, strawberries, shearing, cricket, glow-worms, the hayrick, the dog trial). The rest are season-neutral.
- **Slots:** visits are noon, afternoon, evening and night. Daytime cards have `slot != "night"`; evening and night cards (the soup kitchen and the sleeping cabby in the city; glow-worms, the poacher and the wassail in the country), the mummers never at noon.
- Light on purpose: T1 and the K-cards carry the valley's eeriness (the sevens, the milestone, the well dressing). These lanes are ordinary country life, with no sevens, no hill, no frost, no stars and no bells.

## The year, read back (3; budget 3)

`@pool year_recap` (`trigger: auto`, milestone, `repeat: 1`, priority 40): at the end of each of the first three
years (turns 12, 24, 36), you read the year back in your journal. No other card plays on those turns. The year
cards at turns 13, 25 and 37 look ahead; these look back. The text picks its fragments by:
- friendships (`bonds(friend) + bonds(close)`, and `bonds(partner)` in year 2 and year 3);
- renown (`renown_known()`, `renown_famous()`);
- marks (`gpa >= 3`, year 1);
- whether the main plot has left you a question (`clues(hollowstar) >= 1` / `>= 2`). This names nothing about the
  mystery.

Each recap is a choice between looking back (`mood +small`, `study -tiny`) and looking ahead (`study +small`,
`mood -tiny`).

## Nudges (6; budget 6)

`@pool nudge` (routine, `repeat: 1`): a lead you found and haven't followed for six months (`since(<the card that
found it>) >= 6`), while it can still be followed (you're still in the club or the job, `turn <= 44`). Nudges are
only for side threads. Each one points to the quest's next step without taking it:
- the Ledger's quill (Nessa);
- Footnote's drawer;
- the landlady's two taps;
- the duelling dummy (Lyra);
- the red ink;
- the jar of wind.

The club and job nudges are part of their track (`track:`), as the quest steps they point to are. Dealt as social
cards or at the place, they never came up: the actor's own social cards and the job's shift cards came first.

whose feelings the cast already sets. The start cards are social cards of Corvin and Tobias (place-bound starts
showed in 1 and 5 of 60 games). The circle's exam night waits for an exam month (`defer`). Queue delays use
`within N..M` (an `in N..M` slipped in and parsed as an expression without a lint error; noted in the tracker).

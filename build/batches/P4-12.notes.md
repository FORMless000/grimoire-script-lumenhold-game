# P4-12 — use what you know (part 1), second-hand facts, a sold secret

File: `content/events/knowing.grim` (25 cards), its zh-CN.

## Use what you know (15; budget 30 over P4-12 and P4-13)

`@pool knowing` (`class: routine`, `repeat: 1`, tags people facts): each card has an option, or a better outcome,
that shows only once you know a fact (`when: knows(…)` on the option, so it shows locked until then, or an
`if knows(…)` branch). Most are tied to a place: Isolde's chess (her tower), Nessa ×2 (the Ledger office), Thorne's
hands (lecture tower), Grimsby's nose, Pell's counting, Amaranth, Marisol's spirits, Vey's smell, Brask's asking,
Ilse's stitch (courtyard). The others are social: Kael (the payoff of `kael_until_the_thaw`), Ilse's useless things,
Seraphine's "too loud", Tamsin's failed exam (a letter).
Dominance lint: in every `if knows` card, the else-branch has its own gain (for example `know_nessa_planted`'s else
is `mood +small`, with a matching say).

## Second-hand facts (8; budget 8)

`@pool second_hand` (`trigger: social`, routine, `repeat: 1`): A tells you something about B. Both options
`learn` B's fact; the choice is what you do with it (keep it, pass it on, tell B). The pairs come from canon
relations: Bram→Corvin, Corvin→Bram, Maud→Thorne, Mabel→Grimsby, Grimsby→Mabel, Marisol→Vey, Nessa→Isolde,
Tobias→Mir.

## Cross-system: a sold secret (2; budget 12 over P4-10 and later)

`sell_a_secret` (pool knowing, the village, `renown_known()`): the gossip-sheet printer offers money and renown for
something you know about a friend. It casts any met peer with rel ≥ 15 whose secret you know (Bram's cheating, Tobias's
blight, Lyra's burnout, Corvin's brother). Selling queues `scandal_secret_out` (pool scandal, `actor: npc`) within
2..4 months, likely, carrying the friend.
Written first for Bram only, then made generic: Bram was over the per-NPC cap, and the card was close to
`ledger_bram_story`.

## Sim (economy, 60 games per style: social / celebrity / balanced)

Games in which each card is shown: Nessa's two 42–48, `heard_maud_thorne` 26–41, `heard_nessa_isolde` ~29,
`heard_tobias_mir` 25–33, Pell 16–24, Kael 9–18, Thorne 8–11, most others 1–14. `sell_a_secret` 0/2/0 (Known, a
known secret and a friend at once: rare, as meant).
Three cards were never dealt at first, and were fixed:
- `know_isolde_chess` was placed at the headmistress's tower, which the sims almost never visit (its other card
  shows once in 180 games). It is now Isolde's social card: 19–32.
- The two village second-hand cards (Grimsby on Mabel, Mabel on Grimsby) were social cards for villagers, who are
  rarely the social actor. Each now plays at its own shop (`trigger: random`, `place:`, weight 25) once you've met
  its speaker: 2–6 each.

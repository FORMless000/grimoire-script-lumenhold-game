# P4-02 — notes

Phase 4, batch 2 (`docs/phase4-plan.md`): generic inter-NPC cards, matchmaking, jealousy and what people say
(`content/events/between_hearts.grim`, 17 cards). Uses the pools of P4-01 (`between`, `between_once`) and one new
named condition, `fancy_free(x)` (not your partner or ex, and not sweet on you), for the pairs you match.

## Wiring

- Matching works on the pair's own feelings (`npc_rel a b`). A queued card keeps only the role it was queued with
  (`queue … with a`), so the aftermaths (`between_match_made`, `between_match_soured`) and `between_did_they_tell`
  are about that one person and don't name the other. Taking the credit for a match sets `flag matchmaker`, which
  opens `between_matchmaker`.
- One-offs add `seen(<own id>) == 0` (P4-01's rule); `between_overheard` and `between_rumour_from_you` are capped at
  two showings (`seen(…) < 2`), since they came up to five times a game.

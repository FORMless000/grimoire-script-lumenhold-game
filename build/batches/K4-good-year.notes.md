## Context

- The countryside's side storyline from the plan (`docs/maps-plan.md`, "The plot: The Good Year"). The surface: seven good harvests and everyone glad to see a stranger. The truth (soft lore `good_year`): every seventh year the valley lends a stranger to the Tenant under the hollow hill; the keepers tell themselves the lent come home seven years on, but they sit unaged at its supper for as long as the bargain holds; their coats wear the scarecrows. Keepers: the coaching inn's landlord and the miller; the hedge-witch keeps who she can out with rue; the land agent (K-P2's man in good boots) has bought the old hall and means to improve the bargain for his company's land.
- **What it pays off,** all planted earlier and plot-safe: the harvest board's columns of seven (`market_harvest_board`), the landlord's welcome (`market_inn_welcome`), the scarecrows' coats (`fields_scarecrow_coats`), the dry wheel (`mill_dry_wheel`), the almanac's missing page (`almanac_short_page`, `almanac_pages_counted`), the agent at the Fennick farm (`fennick_good_boots`, `fennick_agent_refused`), the hedge-witch's rue and "mind the inn" (`marsh_witch_stranger`), the satchel's room-seven key in the peat (`moor_inn_key`), and K3's shut door in the hill, which this opens.
- **Shape:** a `story` thread begun by `gy_seventh_year` at the market village. Clues in any order, each a step at its place, counted in `gy_clues` (the guest book, the collars, the miller, the almanac page, the agent's desk at the old hall). Meeting Ottilie Venn (a fern-hunter given room seven) plus two clues: she's gone and the inn says she never came. Then the hedge-witch's truth (`gy_truth`), and the door in the hill stands open at the moors delve's depth 7 (`gy_hill_door_open`, in the `moor_delve` thread). The Tenant's table is a menu: walk out (come back another time), fight it (break the bargain: the lent go free, a thin harvest follows), appeal (the whole valley at its supper for one night every seventh year instead: the lent go free, the luck stays; adept+ unlikely, +1 for each of the guest book, the collars and the almanac page, so the clues pay at the table), or, only with the agent's papers, give it the agent's offer (Hollow, the agent's purse). Aftermath cards for each; `gy_thin_harvest` comes on a visit at least three months after breaking it.
- **Origins:** the start and the hedge-witch's truth branch for those at home in the valley (the neighbours' looks; the hedge origin's grandmother, who kept you out of it with rue every seventh year); the thin harvest reaches the family table.
- **Plot safety:** no `advance`, no arc clues (only the thread's own leads); no frost, no stars, no bells (the plan's "clapperless bell" was dropped for that reason; the miller's tally takes its place). Adults only; the Tenant is hungry for company, not flesh.

## The Tenant (combat/tools/creature-study.mjs, decent classes 10 levels under, foe on medium)

A boss built from the oaks' moves (Root, Bough, Bark, Roar), hp ×2: classes win 5% / 33% / 65% at foe levels 30 / 40 / 50, between the conger (0 / 65 / 88) and the elder oak (0 / 15 / 18). It is one of four ways out of the hall, and losing it leaves you on the moor (`moor_lost`).

# P4-01 — notes

Phase 4, batch 1 (`docs/phase4-plan.md`): generic inter-NPC cards, friction, feuds and cliques
(`content/events/between.grim`, 15 cards). Two `any` roles each, read through `npc_rel`: the player stands between
two classmates who dislike each other, or beside two who are close.

## Wiring

- `@pool between` (trigger social, class routine, repeat 8, weight 7, tags social between) and `@pool between_once`
  (the same, once per game, weight 6) hold everything the cards share.
- New named conditions in `content/core/conds.grim`: `at_odds(a, b)` (npc_rel ≤ −5), `get_along(a, b)` (≥ 5) and
  `classmate_here(x)` (a met, present peer who isn't an outsider). Every card casts its two roles through them.
- Each card moves the pair's own feelings (`npc_rel a b ±tier`) as well as the player's with each of them, so a
  later card in the batch, or a P4-03/04 one-side-specific card, can read what the player did.
- Lint: `npc_rel` accepts cast roles (it rejected `npc_rel a b` before; `tools/grim.js`).

## Critic round 1 (rejected) and the fixes

- **Once cards repeated** (`per: a` makes once per character): whose_idea, not_them, peace_offering, seconds, cipher,
  cover_roll, closed_table and falling_out each add `seen(<own id>) == 0`; the pool's desc says why.
- **falling_out** casts only a pair who get on (`get_along(a, b)`).
- **The hour:** night_roof is `slot: night` (11 of 60 social games). Pinning the supper, vote and roll-call cards to a
  slot starved them (2–7 of 60), so their text no longer fixes the hour ("at mealtime", "today's vote") and they
  favour it with `time:` instead (two_seats and closed_table `noon evening`, dorm_vote `evening night`, cover_roll
  `morning noon afternoon`).
- **whose_idea** is term-only. **seconds**: frost only in winter; "if I lose, say I didn't" (a lie Nessa wouldn't
  ask for) is now "don't let me hear about it".
- inside_joke's label is "What's the joke?"; messenger's lose line answers its own variant, and both cards use
  `{b.theirs}`; two_seats drops "which is a first"; dorm_vote's label is "Propose a middle way".
- **lab_partners** is an alchemy practical: `enrolled(alchemy)`, `study alchemy`.
- **Voice:** Kael is kept out of inside_joke and cipher, Bram out of night_roof (`not npc_is(…)` on both roles).

60 social games after the fixes: every once card at most once a game; repeatables up to 3–4 times (3 variants each).

# P4-09 — scandals

Files: `content/events/scandals.grim`, `locales/zh-CN/events/scandals.lang`; seven `queue` lines added to the
spotlight cards that set them up (spotlight.grim, spotlight_famous.grim, spotlight_legend.grim).

15 cards (budget 15), each a menu (option keys `own`/`deny`/`spin`) in `@pool scandal` (`class: incident`,
`trigger: queued`, `repeat: 1`):
- own it: renown falls (small–medium), a step toward Lumen, the people involved keep faith;
- deny / fight it: a check (deceive, research, appeal, spell at `apprentice+`…`adept`): the story dies, or gets worse
  (renown −medium/−large, trust);
- spin it: the story moves onto someone else: a step toward Hollow (small–medium), a cost to someone, little renown lost.

7 callbacks, queued by the bold choice on a spotlight card (`within a..b`, most `chance unlikely`/`even`; `when:
renown_known()`, so a forgotten star's past stays buried): ledger_lie (spot_ledger_profile, polished), candle_smoke
(spot_endorsement), memoir_book (spot_memoir), flourish_hurt (spot_flourish_named), wrong_notes
(spot_notes_for_sale, took a cut), thornwick_cheat (spot_thornwick_challenge, won), society_dare (spot_fan_circle).
8 any-Famous scandals in `@pool scandal_any`, dealt by `@appointment scandal` (Famous, `chance: slim`) and
`@appointment scandal_legend` (Legend, a second slim roll): about 15% a month while Famous, 28% as a Legend.

taint), "the fall at the Ball" (spot_ball_opening's lose branch is that fall).

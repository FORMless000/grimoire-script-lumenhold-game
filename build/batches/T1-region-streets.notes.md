# T1 region street cards and lodging nights: author's notes

Batch: `content/events/maps/region_streets.grim` (23 cards). Engine/tool: `tools/reach.js` now counts a map's `lodging:` states as gained (the arrival screen grants them), so cards gated on `lodged_rough()` / `lodged_fine()` are reachable.

- **Capital streets** (pool `capital_streets`, `region: capital`, 10 cards) and **countryside lanes** (`country_lanes`, `region: countryside`, 7 cards). Region cards have no place: on the away maps a visit falls back on them once a place's own cards are spent (`src/director.js`, the visit fallback; the away plans' days are four visits, no social/world slot). So they read as "out and about in the city / valley", wherever the visit was. Once each (no `repeat`).
- **Lodging nights**: pools `lodging_nights_city` (`plan: travel_city`) and `lodging_nights_country` (`plan: travel_country`): the travel plans' morning `plan:0-1` card, beside the placeholder `travel_*_morning`. One card per lodging tier: rough (`lodged_rough()`), decent (neither), fine (`lodged_fine()`). Players at home never get them (at home isn't a travel plan).
- **Retrieval** (`retrieve related --draft` per card; candidates read): the ballad-seller's "correct the verses" option repeated `spot_tavern_song` ("Teach them the true verses"); reworked to "Ask him where he got it". Sea fog vs `life_fog_maze` (lake fog, a voice calling: different beat — guiding a blind man who knows the streets by sound). Thunderstorm on the ridge stones and the well-dressing traveller sit beside the valley's seventh-year motifs (`ridge_count_aloud`, `mill_skipping_rhyme`) on purpose, as edge-of-sight hints; nothing explains the custom (that is K4). Wedding vs `company_wedding` (an upstairs wedding job in the city; this is a country procession you're pulled into).
- **Plot safety**: no `find`/`advance`/`close`; eerie beats (the automaton writing your initials, the milestone TO THE SEA, the owl's barn, the thing on the hayloft ladder, the light between the stones) never touch the Hollow Star.
- **Costs**: every option trades; spends and losses are in their says. Alignment only on moral choices (helping the blind man, the bucket chain, the egg woman's seat, catching the thief and letting him go, the wedding gift, the flower seller, standing by pickpockets' marks, answering the preacher honestly).
- **Time fit**: region cards' `time:` only weights them (they have no place's `open:` to contradict). The lodging cards are morning plan cards that tell of the night before, in the present tense like the rest of the game.
